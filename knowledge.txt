COMPONENT: Automotive Air Conditioning System
==================================================

Description:
The vehicle AC system removes heat from cabin air using a compressor,
condenser, expansion valve and evaporator.

SYMPTOMS:

1. Weak cooling during afternoon but normal at night

Possible Causes:

Cause:
Condenser airflow problem

Explanation:
The condenser releases heat from refrigerant. During high temperature
conditions or slow traffic, reduced airflow can prevent proper heat
dissipation and reduce cooling performance.

Diagnostic Checks:
- Check condenser fan operation
- Inspect condenser for dirt or blockage
- Check airflow through condenser

Repair Suggestions:
- Clean condenser surface
- Repair faulty cooling fan
- Remove airflow obstruction


Cause:
Low refrigerant level

Explanation:
Low refrigerant reduces the ability of the AC system to absorb heat.

Diagnostic Checks:
- Measure refrigerant pressure
- Check for refrigerant leaks

Repair Suggestions:
- Repair leakage
- Recharge refrigerant


==================================================

SYMPTOM:

AC works while driving but becomes weak when stopped

Possible Causes:

Cause:
Cooling fan failure

Explanation:
At higher speeds outside airflow cools the condenser. At idle,
the electric fan is required. A failed fan reduces condenser cooling.

Diagnostic Checks:
- Turn AC on and check fan operation
- Inspect fan fuse and relay

Repair Suggestions:
- Replace faulty fan
- Repair electrical connection


==================================================
COMPONENT: Engine Starting System
==================================================

Description:
The starting system includes battery, starter motor and ignition system.

SYMPTOM:

Vehicle does not start

Possible Causes:

Cause:
Weak battery

Explanation:
A low battery cannot provide enough current to turn the starter motor.

Diagnostic Checks:
- Check battery voltage
- Check battery terminals
- Try jump starting

Repair Suggestions:
- Charge battery
- Replace battery if damaged


Cause:
Starter motor problem

Explanation:
The starter motor may fail to rotate the engine.

Diagnostic Checks:
- Listen for clicking sound
- Check starter connections

Repair Suggestions:
- Repair or replace starter motor


==================================================
COMPONENT: Engine Overheating System
==================================================

SYMPTOM:

Engine temperature increases in traffic

Possible Causes:

Cause:
Cooling fan failure

Explanation:
During slow traffic, the radiator requires the cooling fan
to maintain airflow.

Diagnostic Checks:
- Check radiator fan operation
- Check coolant temperature sensor

Repair Suggestions:
- Repair fan system


Cause:
Low coolant level

Explanation:
Insufficient coolant reduces heat transfer from engine.

Diagnostic Checks:
- Check coolant reservoir level
- Inspect coolant leakage

Repair Suggestions:
- Fix leaks
- Refill coolant


==================================================
COMPONENT: Battery and Electrical System
==================================================

SYMPTOM:

Electrical components weak or vehicle battery dies

Possible Causes:

Cause:
Old battery

Explanation:
Battery capacity decreases with age.

Diagnostic Checks:
- Measure battery voltage
- Perform battery load test

Repair Suggestions:
- Replace battery


Cause:
Alternator charging issue

Explanation:
Alternator maintains battery charge while engine runs.

Diagnostic Checks:
- Measure charging voltage
- Inspect alternator belt

Repair Suggestions:
- Repair alternator
