Automotive AC Troubleshooting

Problem:
AC cooling weak during afternoon traffic but works at night.

Possible causes:

1. Condenser airflow problem
Reason:
During traffic, less airflow reaches the condenser.
The AC loses cooling efficiency.

Check:
Inspect condenser fan operation.
Check condenser blockage.

Repair:
Clean condenser.
Repair cooling fan.


2. Low refrigerant

Reason:
Low refrigerant reduces cooling capacity.

Check:
Measure AC pressure.

Repair:
Recharge refrigerant.


Engine overheating

Problem:
Engine temperature increases after driving.

Causes:
Low coolant.
Radiator blockage.
Cooling fan failure.

Checks:
Inspect coolant level.
Check radiator fan.
